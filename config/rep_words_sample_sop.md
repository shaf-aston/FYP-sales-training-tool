# Discovery call SOP: online business mentorship

## Open
- Thank them for booking and check they can hear you
- Ask what they would like help with first

## Discover
- Ask what they want to achieve by making money online
- Ask how much they would need to make each month
- Ask how long they have been thinking about this
- Ask what has been the hardest part to figure out so far
- Ask what happens if nothing changes and they carry on as they are

## Belief
- Ask what they believe about themselves that has held them back
- Ask where they think that belief comes from

## Close
- Present the programme in their words
- Ask: "Do you see yourself jumping in?"
- Ask how they would like to pay, credit or debit
