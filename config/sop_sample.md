# Discovery call SOP: growth coaching

## Open
- Thank them for their time and confirm they have 30 minutes
- Set the agenda: their goals, what's in the way, then whether we can help

## Discover
- Ask what made them book the call
- Ask what they want to achieve in the next 12 months
- Find out what's getting in the way and how long it has been a problem
- Ask what happens if nothing changes this year
- Ask who else is involved in the decision and what budget they have set aside

## Pitch
- Recap their goals in their own words
- Present the 12-week programme tied to the problem they described

## Handle objections
- If they say it's too expensive, acknowledge it and reframe around what the problem is costing them
- If they need to think about it, ask what specifically they need to think through

## Close
- Ask them directly if they want to go ahead
- If they're not ready, book a follow-up call before hanging up
